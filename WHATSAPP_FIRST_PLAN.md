# CAR-AGENTS — WhatsApp-First Improvement Plan

**Purpose:** Make the current CAR-AGENTS system more useful for the founder without major rebuilding.
**Goal:** The founder works from WhatsApp. He gets details, receives leads, and schedules meetings by chat, without opening the portal.

---

## 1. Summary

The current system is a web portal with a Telegram bot attached. The client doesn't want to use a portal. This plan turns that around:

- **WhatsApp becomes the main interface.**
- **The portal becomes a back-office view** that is rarely opened.
- **Leads from WhatsApp and email** are collected in one place and qualified automatically with fixed questions.

Most of the logic already exists (Telegram bot commands, email lead capture, calendar, briefings, voice transcription, contract PDFs, and a WhatsApp sending service). The work is mainly connecting these to WhatsApp, not building new systems.

## 3. Lead Qualification (Fixed Questions)

The questions are **fixed, not generated by AI**. They are the same for WhatsApp and email. This keeps wording consistent, costs nothing per message, and lets the client approve the exact text. AI is used only for the first Buy vs Sell classification, which already exists.

The customer answers by tapping a button (WhatsApp) or replying with a number (email).

### Buyer flow
**Q1. What best describes you?**
1. 🚨 I need a car urgently
2. 🛒 I want to buy soon
3. 👀 I'm just looking / have an enquiry

**Q2. When do you want to buy?** *(only if 1 or 2)*
This week · Within a month · 1–3 months

**Q3. What is your budget?** *(only if 1 or 2)*
Under €20k · €20–50k · €50–100k · Over €100k

### Seller flow
**Q1.** Do you want to sell quickly, within a month, or just get a valuation?
**Q2.** Which car? (make, model and year, or send the registration papers and the OCR reads them)
**Q3.** Rough asking price? *(optional)*

### Scoring table (no AI needed)

| Urgency | Budget given? | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Any | 🔥 Hot |
| Buy soon (within a month) | Yes | 🔥 Hot |
| Buy soon (1–3 months) | Yes | 🟡 Warm |
| Buy soon | No | 🟡 Warm |
| Just looking | Any | ❄️ Cold |

The client can change this table without development work.

### Rules
- Maximum **3 questions** per customer.
- **One reminder** after 24 hours, then the lead is marked Cold.
- **Free-text replies** are saved on the lead for the founder to read. The bot doesn't interpret them.
- **Languages:** German by default, English available.
- **Consent line** in the first message: "By replying you agree we store your details to contact you."
- The **founder is alerted immediately** for Hot leads. Warm leads go in the daily briefing. Cold leads wait in the list.
- Labels are suggestions. The founder can change them.

## 4. Changes Required

### 4.1 Reuse (already exists, only re-routed)
| Existing | Reused for |
|---|---|
| Telegram bot commands and lookups | WhatsApp commands |
| Email poller and Buy/Sell classifier | Same lead flow, now also for WhatsApp |
| Scheduler and briefings | Deliver the morning briefing on WhatsApp |
| Calendar with travel conflict guard | Scheduling by chat |
| Voice transcription | WhatsApp voice notes |
| PDF contract engine and Drive sync | Send contracts from chat |
| WhatsApp sending service | Outbound messages (needs completing for inbound) |

### 4.2 New work
| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Receive WhatsApp messages | A webhook endpoint for incoming messages. |
| Command handling for WhatsApp | Reuse Telegram logic. Only the founder's number is allowed to run commands. |
| Conversation memory per lead | Records which question the lead is on. This is the only new technical piece. |
| Question scripts and scoring table | German and English, kept in one editable place. |
| Hot/Warm/Cold label on leads | One new field, shown in the lead list and alerts. |
| Lead source label (Email / WhatsApp) | Shown in the lead list. |
| Natural-language meeting scheduling | Understand a date and time from a short message and confirm back. |
| WhatsApp message templates | Pre-approved by Meta for alerts, briefings and the first auto-reply. |

### 4.3 Portal changes (small)
- Hide pages the client doesn't use.
- Show the lead label and source.
- Show the qualification answers on the lead detail panel.

### 4.4 Not in scope
Bank feed integration, multi-user roles, and autonomous AI writes from voice. These stay in Phase 2 of the main roadmap.

## 5. Prerequisites and Risks

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Meta Business verification** | Still pending. **No WhatsApp feature works until this is complete.** Start immediately. |
| **WhatsApp number** | Decide whether the business number is used for both customers and the founder, or a separate assistant number. |
| **Message templates** | WhatsApp only allows free messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Alerts, briefings and the first auto-reply need pre-approved templates. Approval takes days. |
| **Security** | Only the founder's number can run commands. All other numbers are treated as customers. |
| **GDPR** | Consent line in the first auto-reply. Store only what is needed. |
| **Fallback** | Keep Telegram running while Meta approval is pending. The logic is shared. |
| **AI scores** | Treat the label as a suggestion. Review "Hot" criteria with the client during the first weeks. |

## 6. Phased Plan

### Phase 0 — Unblock (start immediately, runs in parallel)
- Complete Meta Business verification and set up the WhatsApp number.
- Submit message templates.
- Confirm the founder's phone number.
- Agree question wording and budget ranges with the client.

### Phase 1 — Founder talks to the system (about 1 week)
- Receive WhatsApp messages.
- Commands: deals, leads, customers, summary.
- Alerts and morning briefing on WhatsApp.
- Hide unused portal pages.

**Result:** the founder can stop opening the portal for daily checks.

### Phase 2 — Leads from WhatsApp and qualification (about 1–2 weeks)
- Customer messages become leads.
- Auto-reply with the fixed question flow.
- Hot/Warm/Cold label and the founder alert with a summary.
- Lead source shown in the list.
- Reminder after 24 hours.

**Result:** one lead list from both channels, sorted by quality.

### Phase 3 — Do things from chat (about 1–2 weeks)
- Create and move meetings by chat.
- Voice notes with confirmation.
- Quick actions: convert lead to deal, log hours, send contract.

**Result:** the founder can work from a car lot with only his phone.

### Phase 4 — Polish (after he has used it)
- Reminders for Hot leads with no follow-up.
- Adjust wording and scoring.
- Add commands he asks for.

---

## 7. Decisions Needed From the Client

1. Exact wording of the questions and the budget ranges.
2. What counts as a **Hot** lead (budget, car type, timing)?
3. Should Hot leads alert him at any time, including at night?
4. For sellers, are valuation-only requests worth chasing?
5. One WhatsApp number or two?
6. Which actions does he use most on his phone: deals, leads or scheduling? That one is built first.
